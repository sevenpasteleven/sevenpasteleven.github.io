# Overview.
Welcome to my research portfolio! My name is Henrik Kaiser and I'm currently postdoctoral researcher at the IDA Lab, which is part of the Paris Lodron University of Salzburg, Austria. I began my study of mathematics in 2012 at Justus-Liebig University of Giessen, Germany, where I eventually finished with my PhD in 2023.

## Research areas.
My main research areas are inverse problems, especially in statistics, mainly deconvolution. Besides, I work on topics related to copula theory, integral transforms, special functions and asymptotic expansions.

## Programming skills.
I like programming in Python, however, in the past I also worked with R.
